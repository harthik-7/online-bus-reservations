Our Inception

A Vision to Transform Indian Road Travel

Established in 2018 under the visionary leadership of Harthik Raj, our brand emerged from a simple observation: road travelers deserved more than cramped seats, unpredictable delays, and opaque fares.

Starting with a modest fleet of 10 modern coaches connecting Chennai and Bangalore, HARTHIKRAJ has evolved into a premier national network operating over 2,500 daily departures across South and Central India.

We have engineered every touchpoint—from our seamless one-click seat reservation platform to custom-built sleeper berths and telematics-powered safety control rooms—to make long-distance bus travel pure joy.
